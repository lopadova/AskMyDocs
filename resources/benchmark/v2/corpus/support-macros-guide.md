# Support macros guide

How the customer support team uses saved replies (macros) in the helpdesk.

## Response targets

The first reply must be sent within 4 business hours of the ticket being
created. Priority 2 tickets must be resolved within 2 business days.
Priority 1 tickets (order lost, wrong delivery address after dispatch) are
handled by the duty agent immediately.

## Common macros

- **Delay apology**: use when the carrier has a known delay; add the tracking link.
- **Return instructions**: explains the return portal and the RMA code.
- **Warranty claim**: collects photos and the purchase date.

## Tags

Every ticket must carry one tag for the topic (delivery, return, invoice,
account) and one for the channel (email, chat, phone).

## Escalation to tier 2

Escalate when the agent cannot answer after 30 minutes of investigation, or
when the customer asks for compensation. Write the case summary in three
lines: what happened, what was tried, what is requested.
