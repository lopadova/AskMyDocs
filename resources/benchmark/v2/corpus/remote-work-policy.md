# Remote work policy

Applies to office-based employees of Northwind Logistics. Warehouse roles
are not eligible because they require physical presence.

## How many days

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. The remaining days are
spent at the office, and teams agree on shared anchor days.

## Core hours

Everyone is reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 CET on remote days.

## New hires

New hires work from the office during their first 3 months to complete
onboarding, then become eligible for the remote schedule.

## Equipment and allowance

The company provides a laptop and a monitor. A home-office allowance of
EUR 40 per month is paid with the payroll.

## Security

Remote work requires the corporate VPN and a managed device. Public Wi-Fi is
allowed only through the VPN.
