# Refund Policy (2023 edition, superseded)

> Superseded by DEC-REFUND-002 in January 2024. Kept for audit purposes only.

This decision defined when and how much Northwind Logistics refunded on a
returned order line.

## Eligibility windows

- **Days 0-14 after delivery:** full refund of the line total.
- **Days 15-30 after delivery:** partial refund, capped at 25% of the line total.
- **Day 31 onwards:** no commercial refund of any kind.

## Approval

Any refund above EUR 200 needed a second approver from the finance team.

## Payout

The money returned to the original payment method within 10 business days
of the approval. Shipping fees were never refunded, not even on a full refund.

## Credit notes

Every refund generated a credit note whose VAT was rounded line by line.
