# Billing FAQ

Answers for the customer success team about invoices and payments.

## When is an invoice issued?

An invoice is issued on the day the order ships, not when it is placed.
Electronic invoices are delivered to the tax authority gateway and copied
to the customer portal.

## What are the payment terms?

Standard terms are 30 days net from the invoice date. Customers on
prepaid plans are invoiced at the time of shipment and pay by card.

## What happens if a client pays late?

A late fee of 1.5% per month on the overdue amount applies from the first
day after the due date. The fee can be waived once per year by a finance
manager.

## Which payment methods are accepted?

SEPA direct debit, credit card and bank transfer. Cash is not accepted.

## How do I get a duplicate invoice?

Customers download copies from the portal under Documents. Support can
resend a PDF but cannot edit an issued invoice; corrections need a credit
note.
